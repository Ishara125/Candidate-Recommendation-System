Evaluation and Analysis Summary
================================

Top-K value used: 5

Overall Evaluation Results
--------------------------
Average Precision: 0.15
Average Recall: 0.25
Average F1-score: 0.188

Strengths
---------
- The system can rank candidates based on CV and job description similarity.
- The system works well when CVs and job descriptions contain similar skill keywords.
- The recommendation output is easy to evaluate using Precision, Recall, and F1-score.

Weaknesses
----------
- The system depends heavily on the words used in CVs and job descriptions.
- TF-IDF may not understand the deeper meaning of skills or experience.
- The evaluation dataset is small, so the result may not represent real-world accuracy fully.

Future Improvements
-------------------
- Increase the number of CVs and job descriptions.
- Use advanced NLP models such as BERT or Sentence Transformers.
- Add separate matching for skills, education level, and work experience.
